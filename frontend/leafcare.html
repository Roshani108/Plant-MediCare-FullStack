Expert Knowledge

Plant Care Wisdom

💧

The Finger Test

Push finger 2 inches into soil. Only water when dry at that depth. Overwatering kills more plants than underwatering.

☀️

Light Reading

"Bright indirect light" = 3–5 feet from a window. Most houseplants thrive here, never in direct sun.

🧴

Neem Oil Cure-All

2ml neem oil per litre water treats most fungal diseases, mites and aphids organically.

✂️

Prune for Power

Remove yellow or dead leaves immediately — they drain energy and harbour fungal spores.

🪣

Drainage First

Always use pots with drainage holes. Standing water for 30+ minutes creates root rot conditions.

🌡️

Temperature Shock

Most plants suffer below 13°C. Keep away from AC vents, cold windows and heaters.
